Three areas I keep coming back to across every project I take on.

Full-Stack Development

Building complete web applications end to end. That covers data models and APIs as well as the interface people interact with. My DUT clinic booking platform is the clearest example. It scored 98 out of 100 for solving a real scheduling problem for the university.
