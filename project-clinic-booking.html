Clinic Booking App

A school clinic appointment booking platform built for Durban University of Technology. It scored 98 out of 100 as a coursework submission and solves a real scheduling problem for students on campus.

Add Live Site Link Add Code Link

The problem

Students at DUT needed a simple way to book clinic appointments without calling or visiting in person. Staff needed a clear view of who was booked in and when.

What it does

  • Patients can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments
  • Staff get an admin view of the day's schedule
  • Automated confirmations reduce no-shows
